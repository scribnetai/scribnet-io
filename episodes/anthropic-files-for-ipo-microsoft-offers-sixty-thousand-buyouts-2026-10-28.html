Anthropic Files for IPO, Microsoft Offers Sixty Thousand Buyouts

October 28, 2026 · 7:23 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Anthropic files S-1 for Nasdaq IPO as ANT (filed Mon Oct 26)**: H1 2026 revenue $16.3B (vs $1.16B a year earlier, ~14x); net loss $80.7B, mostly a $72.4B non-cash fair-value charge on convertible notes/derivatives/SAFEs; operating loss $693M, with Q2 GAAP operating profit $974M; adjusted operating profit $1.6B; compute commitments ~$265.6B at June 30; founders' Class F holds 50.1% of vote; share count and price range left blank, registration not yet effective. Same day, four firms announced $63B financing to lease Broadcom chips/networking to Anthropic. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Microsoft offers voluntary buyouts to ~60,000 employees (announced Mon Oct 26; Reuters)**: More than a quarter of its ~223,000 global workforce; level 70 and below in participating teams in the US, Canada, UK, India; Xbox, Windows + Devices, Copilot, AI experiences, finance, HR in scope, while security, marketing, and data-center groups largely excluded; no departure target announced; framed against a forecast $175B in calendar-2026 capex; Q1 results due after close Wed Oct 28.
  • **Apple launches M6 MacBook Pro and iMac (announced Tue Oct 27; Reuters)**: 14-inch MacBook Pro from $1,999 (up to 30% faster graphics, 25% faster AI vs M5, up to 24-hour battery); 24-inch iMac from $1,499 (skips M5, up to 5x faster on-device AI vs M4); M6 is Apple's first 2nm chip (already in Mac mini since August); preorders Oct 28 in 35 countries/regions, availability Nov 6; lands a day before Apple's Oct 28 "Pro and Tell" event, where Bloomberg reports a first touchscreen MacBook Pro is expected.
  • **Oracle expands AI chip menu at AI World (announced Oct 27)**: Nvidia Vera Rubin deployments to multi-gigawatt scale; first hyperscaler outside Google to offer Google TPUs (2027); Cerebras Inference planned for OCI in 2027 (Cerebras shares +10.1% to $119.31); SambaNova also planned; reporting mentions OpenAI's Jalapeno chip; OCI president says not a pivot away from Nvidia.
  • **OpenAI makes GPT-6.1 Terra generally available (Oct 27)**: Faster, lower-cost model for computer-use, visual reasoning, and coding; ~50% API price cut for agentic computer-use; Microsoft integrating into Foundry, GitHub Copilot evaluating it.
  • **Security roundup (Oct 27)**: CISA adds Node2Shell (Google Brotli flaw, CVE-2026-111497, CVSS 9.2; fixes released Oct 21; federal deadline Nov 10; honeypot exploit attempts logged) and Adobe Commerce PolyShell (CVE-2026-48356, CVSS 9.3/9.6 in differing reports; hundreds of stores breached via Magento CMS block loader, activity since at least July 2025) to KEV; attackers begin probing a newly patched critical Oracle EBS SQL-injection flaw (CVE-2026-101767, CVSS 9.8, October CPU) on Oct 27; Tailscale 1.104.4 patches Tailworm SSH socket-forwarding flaws (four bulletins TS-2026-014 to -017; forwarding now as logged-in user, not root; no in-the-wild exploitation reported; Headscale not yet fixed) — genuinely new angle vs the Oct 26 episode, which covered the flaws as unpatched.
  • **Quick hit**: Anduril + Qualcomm unveil BlueChip, a US-designed/assembled rugged Snapdragon X laptop for government/defense/first responders (90%+ US/allied parts cost, orders in 2027, ~20% premium expected).
