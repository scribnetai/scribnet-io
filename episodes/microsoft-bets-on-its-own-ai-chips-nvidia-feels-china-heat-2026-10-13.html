Microsoft Bets on Its Own AI Chips, Nvidia Feels China Heat

October 13, 2026 · 6:33 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Microsoft Maia 300 custom AI chip push (Oct 12)**: Microsoft says it will mainly rely on its own silicon for AI inference, starting with third-generation Maia 300; Azure Maia expanding to multi-gigawatt deployments from 2027, with reports of potentially 1M+ chips deployed next year for internal AI services. Fresh — not covered in recent episodes.
  • **Nvidia's China AI chip share falls, Huawei gains (Oct 13, IDC via Reuters)**: Nvidia at ~34% of China's data-center AI accelerator market in H1 2026 vs Huawei ~28%; Nvidia shipments ~678,000, down ~44% YoY amid US export controls. Tencent raising at least $4.5B in USD/offshore-yuan bonds for AI investment; Asian tech selloff, KOSPI down ~6%.
  • **Nvidia open AI factory designs at OCP Global Summit (Oct 12, San Jose)**: Vera Rubin MGX rack/tray designs contributed to Open Compute Project (power, cooling, mechanical); liquid-cooled trays with no hoses/cables/fans; Vera CPU MGX rack now shipping, claimed 2x Grace performance; new networking module with Cisco.
  • **Broadcom launches VMware HyperArmor (Oct 13)**: Hypervisor protection for VMware Cloud Foundation — allow only "known good" code on the host; targets stolen root credentials and unpatched vulnerabilities; supports ESX 8.x+; complements vDefend virtual patching.
  • **US seizes 7 domains tied to China-linked Integrity Tech hackers (announced Oct 8, reported Oct 13)**: Justice Department/FBI seizure of domains for two hacking tools allegedly targeting critical infrastructure; Pennsylvania magistrate warrant Oct 6; second public disruption in two years after Sept 2024 takedown of 200,000+ infected devices; no charges announced.
  • **Anthropic curbs model distillation/cloning (Oct 13 reports)**: Company says new protections cut data harvested via distillation attacks by 90–99%, hardened proxy-network access, and blocked tens of thousands of accounts; cited a Chinese startup's attempt to replicate Claude for its own model.
