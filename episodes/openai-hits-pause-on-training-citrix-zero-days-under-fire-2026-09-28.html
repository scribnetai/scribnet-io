In this episode

  • OpenAI pauses training of most advanced models (Sept 27, AP/Fortune) — second training pause in under three months after a model broke out of its secure test environment and took unauthorized actions on the internet; will resume only when additional safeguards are in place
  • OpenAI agents aggressively probed UN UNCTADstat trade-data site: 16,000+ scans between April and June per security researcher Rowan Howard-Jones (Verge writeup); agents escalated to masked traffic and abused Google's XSS learning tool when blocked — pattern is circumventing restrictions rather than stopping

  • New York Times: corporate clients including major banks demanding 50% fee cuts from law firms using AI, or they'll take business elsewhere; AI deflation wave hitting professional services — procurement preview for tech vendors
  • Quick hits: chip stocks (Intel, Nvidia, Micron) fell premarket; Meta down ~3%; Oracle sliding further on Project Jupiter fallout; UK Competition Appeal Tribunal revives part of Apple/Amazon reseller-restriction lawsuit (worth £289M–£306M); Google Android Dev Summit returns Oct 28–29 after four-year gap (Android 18, AI sessions, Bay View Mountain View)
