It raised the floor for its twenty twenty-six capital spending to between one hundred thirty-five billion and one hundred forty-five billion dollars. And it announced a plan to issue a new class of non-voting shares to help raise the money. Meta's stock fell about seven percent on the day.

Alex: Narrower, based on what traders actually did. Money rotated into defensive stocks like food, utilities, and health care, and the average stock outside the A I trade actually rose on the day. Investors are not saying demand for A I is fake. They are asking who pays for the buildout, and how fast the returns show up.

Jordan: Which is a perfect bridge to story two, because while American investors were questioning A I spending, Chinese investors were doubling down on it.

Jordan: Who is writing checks that size for a Chinese A I lab?

Alex: Reported backers include C A T L, the world's biggest battery maker, and Tencent, the company behind WeChat, among more than fifteen investors. Founder Liang Wenfeng told investors in September that DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate had reached one billion dollars, more than double the figure reported in the middle of the year, driven mostly by companies paying to access its models.

Railway paused deployments across the whole platform, invalidated login sessions and storage credentials, and by Thursday it had invalidated every Railway A P I token in existence.

Jordan: What is Railway telling customers to do?

It believes this was part of a broader pattern of attacks on platform companies aimed at stealing A I provider keys.

Jordan: The practical lesson writes itself. Alex: It is about debt arithmetic. E A went private two months ago in a fifty-five billion dollar buyout led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund.
