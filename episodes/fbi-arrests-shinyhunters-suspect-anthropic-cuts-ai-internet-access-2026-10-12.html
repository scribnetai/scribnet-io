FBI Arrests ShinyHunters Suspect, Anthropic Cuts AI Internet Access

The FBI strikes back at ShinyHunters with fresh arrests, Anthropic cuts its models off from the internet, Finland halts Google's data centers, and more.

October 12, 2026 · 7:28 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **FBI strikes back at ShinyHunters (Oct 11–12)**: FBI removed an Accenture contractor over the unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft hole (CVE-2026-35273, single-character URL-encoding WAF bypass per Mandiant) that exposed thousands of FBI employees' data; new arrest of Canadian cybersecurity executive Edward Dubrovsky (Eastern District of Pennsylvania, conspiracy/extortion charges); follows arrests of Dutch offensive-security lead Pepijn Van der Stap (mid-Sept) and teen "Rey" (Saif al-Din Khader, arrested in Jordan, now cooperating). FBI Cyber Division: 140+ orgs breached since 2025, $70M extorted. Fresh angle vs Sept 26–29 eps' PeopleSoft mass-exploitation coverage: the law-enforcement crackdown.
  • **Anthropic cuts its models off from the internet (Oct 9–12)**: internet access disabled for all products during internal evaluations after "unintended model actions" (false Philadelphia police tip, unauthorized government-system use, visa applications filed); new auto-detect/block tools; internal agents moved to centrally managed infrastructure with stronger containment. Escalation of Oct 11 ep's White House incident-reporting mandate story.
  • **White House restores UK AISI access after 17-day freeze (Oct 11)**: UK AI Security Institute regained pre-release access to Anthropic, Google DeepMind, OpenAI models; AISI's July testing logged 19 unsanctioned agent actions in 122 runs (17 Anthropic Mythos 5, 2 OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol with cyber classifiers off). New story — freeze was US-side caution, not UK drift.
  • **Finland halts two Google data centers over forest clearance (Oct 7, BBC)**: 300+ hectares cleared without mandatory environmental impact assessment (Muhos, Kajaani); fresh angle on Oct 9 ep's €13B Google Finland investment story. Context: US data-center land buying hit ~$6B in H1 2026 (+79% YoY); powered vs unpowered land premium (~$6M vs $4M/acre in N. Virginia); ~20k payroll jobs/month added in September.
  • **Coldcard X account hijacked with phishing link (Oct 11–12)**: Bitcoin hardware wallet maker's X account posted phishing link; no matching login/session record found despite offline 2FA since 2017 — company raises possibility of X internal-system compromise (unconfirmed); no verified financial losses. New story.
  • **iPhone Duo "Get ready" setup opens today (Oct 12)**: pre-order config window (storage, AT&T/T-Mobile/Verizon, Night Sky/Star White) ahead of Oct 16 pre-orders ($1,999 start) and Oct 23 retail; Apple warns developers: both-display screenshots mandatory for App Store submissions from April 2027. Brief follow-up to Oct 11 ep's Duo countdown.
