SpaceX Seals Forty Billion GPU Lease, Authenticator Flaw Patched

October 27, 2026 · 7:25 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **SpaceX formalizes its $40B GPU lease (SEC filing Mon Oct 26; Apollo-led, Goldman Sachs structuring)**: Binding commitment letter signed Oct 24; Apollo entity buys Nvidia Vera Rubin chips and related equipment and leases them to SpaceX. Deal expected to close before year-end, leases starting H1 2027; each batch leased ~5–5.25 years, quarterly payments in advance, total principal ~$40B. SpaceX calls it the largest committed GPU financing to date. Fresh angle vs Oct 26 episode, which covered only Musk's unconfirmed "closed" claim.
  • **The neocloud divide: Musk vs Huang, JPMorgan sides with the giants**: Musk warns 200+ neoclouds face "guppy-level death and consolidation" as debt markets shut for firms without non-AI revenue; Huang says demand exceeded expectations. JPMorgan: hyperscale operators remain healthy, AI debt markets more selective but no broad financing crisis. Analyst Jeff Pu: SpaceX wanted 30,000 Vera Rubin NVL72 racks, allocated 20,000 — still 2027's biggest buyer (~one-third of demand); Foxconn reportedly took ~45% of SpaceX data-center assembly orders.
  • **Naver completes $9B AI infrastructure deal with Brookfield (announced Oct 27)**: Clears the way for ~$1B Nvidia investment in Naver. Brookfield SPV to build up to $9B in compute infrastructure by 2028; Naver Cloud to offer AI data-center services; Nvidia supplies GPUs and collaborates on next-gen DSX platform.
  • **Microsoft Authenticator Android flaw disclosed Oct 27 (Enclave research; fixed Sept 8, CVE-2026-80097, CVSS 8.6)**: Rogue Android app could silently steal tokens to read/send mail, files, calendar, SharePoint, Teams messages, and alter sign-in methods including passkeys. Requires tricking the user into installing a malicious app. Fix: update to Authenticator 6.2606.4246 or later.
  • **Apple patches 304 vulnerabilities (Oct 26) + iOS 27.2 rolls out**: Fixes include a previously exploited CoreGraphics flaw, unauthorized camera access, Keychain attacks, Gatekeeper/privacy bypasses. iOS 27.2 adds new Siri AI features, contextual Messages suggestions (previewed at WWDC 2026), SynthID expansion in Photos/Image Playground, and iPhone Duo features (Apple Pencil support, Edge Light).
  • **Quick hits**: AI data-center equipment thefts in the US pass $100M in verified losses since June (Verisk CargoNet); PhantomTac research attack tricks robots' magnetic touch sensors with sub-millimeter tags (95%+ success in lab grasping tests, ACM CCS 2026); FCC begins blocking gadgets with surveillance chips, first enforcement — two gadgets with HiVision (Huawei subsidiary) silicon.
