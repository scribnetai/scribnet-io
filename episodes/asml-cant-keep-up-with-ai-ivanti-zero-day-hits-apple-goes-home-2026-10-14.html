Fresh.
  • **Apple Welcome Home launch (Oct 13)**: HomeView 7-inch smart display $299 (tabletop + wall-mount), pre-orders open, Nov 13 launch, recognizes up to 6 users, Siri AI; HomePod mini 2 $149, Oct 30, Siri AI via later update; Apple TV 4K 4th gen $299, A19 chip, Nov 13; Apple Home Collection cameras/lock/thermostats/sensors with LG and Schneider Electric from December.
