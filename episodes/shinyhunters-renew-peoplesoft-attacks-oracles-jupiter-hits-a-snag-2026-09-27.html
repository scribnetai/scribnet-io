ShinyHunters Renew PeopleSoft Attacks, Oracle's Jupiter Hits a Snag

Alex and Jordan cover ShinyHunters' renewed mass exploitation of Oracle PeopleSoft, Oracle's Project Jupiter data-center delay, OpenAI's DevDay GPT-6 Cyber preview, and new chip plays from Xiaomi and China's CXMT.

September 27, 2026 · 6:28 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • ShinyHunters renew mass exploitation of Oracle PeopleSoft (Mandiant report, Sept 26): attackers evaded WAF-only defenses on unpatched systems; dozens of systems hit across higher education, healthcare, agriculture, transportation, government; FBI personnel data theft claim uncorroborated, FBI investigating; Canvas/Instructure 275M-record claim as group context
  • Astrana Health discloses material breach via SEC 8-K (filed Sept 23): vishing/social engineering with spoofed corporate caller ID; third healthcare tech firm this month after Veradigm and Nutex
  • Oracle Project Jupiter force-majeure notice (Sept 24): 2.45 GW New Mexico data-center campus (Blue Owl STACK Infrastructure, built for OpenAI) running ~1 year behind 2028 target; Oracle preserves argument to delay payments; Oracle/Blue Owl shares fell ~3.5%; AI data-center financing risk in the open
  • OpenAI DevDay preview (Sept 29, Fort Mason, SF): GPT-6 Cyber preview (fourth security model of 2026 after 5.4/5.5/5.6 Cyber), in Daybreak Red alpha; enterprise security orchestration platform with gateway architecture and real-time model-behavior monitoring; Ultrafast API mode spotted by TestingCatalog (Standard/Fast/Ultrafast tiers, Cerebras-powered, up to 750 tokens/sec, ~14x faster); Fortune says a dozen+ products possible
  • Xiaomi MiMo 2.6: 1.02T-parameter mixture-of-experts model with fully open weights under MIT license; RL training run live-streamed, reported ~$2.62M cost
  • CXMT G5 DRAM: fifth-gen DRAM platform in mass production at 11.95nm via quadruple patterning (no EUV); ~50% more dies per wafer — China manufacturing workaround story
  • Quick hits: NetApp moving to acquire PEAK:AIO (parallel-NFS AI storage to keep GPUs fed; roots in Los Alamos/Carnegie Mellon); ABB Infinitus 800 VDC power portfolio + Delta 800 VDC distribution with up to 3 MW liquid cooling for 1 MW racks; Axelera Europa 629 TOPS at 45 W landing in validated Dell/Supermicro servers; Samsung One UI 9 launches Sept 28 (Thailand first, Galaxy S25 series + foldables); iPhone Duo ($2,000 foldable) squeezes Korean suppliers — Samsung/LG Display earnings forecasts cut amid won appreciation
