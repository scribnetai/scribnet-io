OpenAI's $20B Revenue Miss Rocks AI Stocks, Japan Cloud Paralyzed

OpenAI's revenue shortfall sends AI stocks tumbling; ransomware knocks SoftBank's IDCF Cloud dark for 495 customers; FBI makes new ShinyHunters arrests.

October 10, 2026 · 7:21 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **OpenAI $20B revenue-expectation gap triggers AI stock selloff (Oct 9, FT/NYT/Bloomberg)**: OpenAI told investors annualized revenue is approaching $50B vs the $70B figure the market assumed; $70B number apparently never came from OpenAI — apples-to-oranges comparison with Anthropic's gross-revenue number. Nasdaq -1.25% (worst since mid-Aug), Nasdaq 100 -1.4%, semiconductor index -3.4%; Nvidia ~3%, Intel ~5.3%, Oracle ~5.5%, CoreWeave ~8%. NYT: OpenAI projecting ~$13B 2026 revenue, breakeven at $100B not until 2029; Altman disputes. Oracle reportedly may reconsider planned $30B OpenAI investment; Oracle reportedly trucking natural gas to server farms for power-bottleneck workaround. Fresh: concrete revenue-data selloff vs Oct 3 ep's Burry crash-call opining.
  • **IDCF Cloud ransomware — 495 customers dark, data likely unrecoverable (Oct 7-8, IDC Frontier/Kyodo/Nikkei)**: SoftBank subsidiary IDC Frontier; East Japan Region 1 (Shirakawa, Fukushima), ~3:40 AM JST Oct 7; 495 companies and local governments lost service; Ibaraki prefecture + prefectural police websites down; Nissui logistics halted (~1,200 business partners affected); JR East/JR Kyushu say up to 7.4M email records may have leaked. Third report: customer data in four zones unlikely retrievable — rebuild from own backups. Attacker (unconfirmed) claims 7-minute breach: 239 hypervisors, 225 databases (3.6 PB), 16,000 VM disks, 554,153 snapshots wiped. Framed as cloud-concentration risk, not a single-company breach.
  • **FBI arrests another ShinyHunters suspect (Oct 9, USA Today/Fox/NYT)**: Canadian citizen arrested in Pennsylvania in FBIJobs.gov personnel-data theft probe; follows Sept 29 Dutch arrest of alleged leader (flash-bang raid, per Reuters). FBI assuming all current/former employees exposed (names, addresses, SSNs, assignments, family names); breach via third-party vendor. Leatherman: network allegedly breached 140+ organizations, $70M+ in extortion. Companion trend: Holland & Knight + Squire Patton Boggs disclosed breaches to VT AG (SSNs exposed); Nelson Mullins hit with class action — law firms as soft-target trend.
  • **South Korean megachurch breaches (Oct 9, Tech Insider)**: Yoido Full Gospel (~850K members) and Sarang Community Church (960K hit) investigating suspected attacks; early signs of AI-automated attacker activity. Continuation of Korea AI-attack thread from Oct 9 ep (CrowdStrike bank breaches). Related: open-source Chinese AI pentest tool ARTEX reported in use against South Korean banks.
  • **Quick hits**: Tab personal AI assistant (Terrasoft) emerges from stealth at $300M valuation 6 weeks post-launch (iMessage/WhatsApp task delegation, 5.9/10 benchmark, ranked 21st; Instinct at $10B, Meta/OpenAI agents); "Midnight Mimosa" malware pre-installed on budget Android phones in 150+ countries; three country-code domain registries hijacked to forge Google certificates; ASOS breach traced to compromised AI marketing vendor; Cognizant "Activation Gap" study — 80% of employees want more AI use at work, 5% productivity gain could add ~$6T to global GDP.
