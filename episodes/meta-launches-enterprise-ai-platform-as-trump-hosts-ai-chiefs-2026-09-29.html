Meta Launches Enterprise AI Platform as Trump Hosts AI Chiefs

Alex and Jordan cover Meta's new enterprise AI business, the White House AI summit with Amodei, Zuckerberg, Huang and Karp, a critical F5 BIG-IP zero-day, the Citrix patch deadline, and Bain's six trillion dollar AI revenue warning.

September 29, 2026 · 6:06 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • Meta Enterprise Platform launch (Sept 29): new business line selling Muse, Business Agent, Muse API, Muse Code plus data-center capacity to enterprises; Zuckerberg calls it the "next major pillar"; ex-MongoDB CEO CJ Desai hired to run it; takes on Microsoft, Salesforce, AWS/Azure and neoclouds
  • White House AI summit (Sept 29): Trump meets Amodei, Zuckerberg, Huang, Karp, Brockman plus Speaker Johnson (Musk possible) amid rogue-agent incidents; follows OpenAI training pause; researchers warn automating AI research could trigger "intelligence explosion"
  • F5 BIG-IP zero-day CVE-2026-94127 (CVSS 9.8, APM heap buffer overflow, exploited pre-patch, 23k+ customers incl. 48 of Fortune 50); fits pattern with Citrix NetScaler edge-device flaws
  • Citrix NetScaler follow-up: CISA KEV patch deadline Sept 30; 50,000+ exposed instances (Palo Alto Networks); earliest exploitation Sept 24 (GreyNoise); public PoC available; patching does not remove persistence
  • ShinyHunters expands Oracle PeopleSoft mass-exploitation with WAF bypass, hundreds of orgs hit
  • Kiteworks lifts precautionary shutdown advisory after federal zero-day warning; no confirmed compromise
  • Bain 7th Global Technology Report: $6T annual revenue needed by 2031 to fund AI compute; AI compressed typical cyberattack from ~4 weeks to 18 hours
  • Pearson acquires Workera (AI-native skills verification for enterprise AI fluency)
