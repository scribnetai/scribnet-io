AMD's AI Blowout, Apple's Second-Day Slide, Barracuda's Own Boxes

AMD's record AI quarter steadies the chip trade while Apple slides on margin guidance, Anthropic's twenty-year compute bill comes into focus, and two security tools need patching themselves.

November 04, 2026 · 6:58 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **AMD's record Q3 2026 (reported Tue Nov 3)**: Revenue $13.4B (+45%), data center revenue $8.55B (+97% YoY, ~64% of total) on EPYC and Instinct MI450 demand; Helios rack systems already running workloads at Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Oracle; Q4 guidance ~$17B beat expectations; stock +8.9%, Nasdaq led higher by AI-infrastructure names (Arista, Super Micro also beat). Fresh — Nov 3 episode only flagged AMD earnings in the week ahead.
  • **Apple follow-through: shares slide despite the record quarter; Siri AI Developer Preview opens (Nov 3)**: Market focused on holiday-quarter margin guidance of 45–46% (memory-chip costs, Siri/AI spend) rather than the beat. New angle: Siri AI Developer Preview in the iOS/iPadOS/macOS/visionOS 27.3 betas lets any developer integrate apps with Siri AI. Follow-through, not a re-cover: Nov 3 episode covered the results themselves.
  • **Anthropic's compute bill: ~$895B in commitments through 2045 (same amended filing as Nov 3 episode)**: Signed purchase commitments and leases, almost all for compute/infrastructure, with plans to more than double capacity next year. New angle — Nov 3 episode covered the filing's self-improvement warning, not the commitment figure.
  • **Barracuda's own appliances hit by critical flaws (disclosed Nov 3)**: CVE-2026-116606 (CVSS 9.3, pre-auth authorization bypass on Email Security Gateway) chained with CVE-2026-111560 (CVSS 8.6, command injection) can yield root access across ESG, WSG, WAF, ADC, and Message Archiver; patches released. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Docker Sandboxes symlink escape fixed (v0.51.0, Nov 3)**: AI coding agents could plant a symlink in an approved workspace so a later copy command read or overwrote host files, including cloud credentials and SSH keys. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Quick hits / week ahead**: Google expands Guided Learning into Classroom globally and Search, and will switch off AI tools for K-12 students early next year unless admins opt in (opting out of AI entirely also loses Google Search); European supermarket refrigeration attacked again Nov 2–3 across at least nine countries via legacy Danfoss controllers (default passwords, unpatched units, factory reset insufficient); monthly jobs report Fri; heavy earnings day ahead.
