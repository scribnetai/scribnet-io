Anthropic Sets IPO Price, Google Drops Gemini 4 Carbon

Anthropic sets its IPO range at 70 to 78 dollars a share ahead of a November 19 listing, Google launches its strongest model yet in Gemini 4 Carbon, and Patch Tuesday brings an exploited Outlook zero-day plus a critical Xen Orchestra bypass.

November 11, 2026 · 6:18 · with Alex & Jordan
