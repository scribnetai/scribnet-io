Haiku 5.5 Cuts AI Prices 90%, Oracle Breach Exposes 20 Million

Anthropic slashes Haiku prices roughly ninety percent, the Oracle Health breach balloons to nearly twenty million victims, and the FBI warns of a massive FortiBleed campaign.

October 08, 2026 · 5:43 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Oracle Health breach balloons to nearly 20M (Oct 5-8, Bloomberg/Texas AG)**: legacy Cerner servers (never migrated to Oracle Cloud after the $28.3B 2022 acquisition), stolen customer credentials, data copied to remote server; SSNs, addresses, diagnoses, meds, test results; ~3M Texans; Oracle knew Feb 2025, notified customers Mar 2025, spent 18+ months not disclosing scale; class action alleged 6M — real number 3x that; Oracle declined comment. Fresh angle: the true-scale revelation (Oct 5 ep covered the different Pentagon breach)
  • **FortiBleed campaign (Oct 8, FBI + Secret Service joint warning)**: 86,644+ Fortinet FortiGate firewalls/SSL VPN gateways across 194 countries; no new vuln — credential stuffing/spraying/cracking with creds from breaches and infostealer logs; attackers create new admin accounts for persistence, sell access; scale revealed when attackers accidentally exposed their own backend server. Distinct from the Oct 2 ep's FortiMail zero-day
  • **Claude Haiku 5.5 launch (Oct 8)**: third in the 5.5 family after Opus 5.5 (covered Oct 6) and Sonnet 5.5; $0.10/$0.50 per M tokens under 100K (~90% cut vs Haiku 4.5); 1M-token context window (5x); new adjustable effort setting (cost vs intelligence dial); live on Claude Platform + AWS/Google Cloud/Azure; matches OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna pricing — model price war escalation
  • **Google SynthID Detector global rollout (Oct 8)**: detects AI-generated images, video, audio worldwide; the public-detector piece that makes invisible watermarking (OpenAI EU text watermarks, SynthID embeddings) actually enforceable
  • **Quick hits**: Cisco drops 18 fixes in one day incl. 3x CVSS-9.8 unauthenticated RCE in NX-OS NGOAM on Nexus 3000/9000 (not yet exploited); Atlassian CVE-2026-21589 now under active exploitation hours after watchTowr PoC (Jira/Confluence/Bitbucket DC); ransomware crews targeting backup infrastructure to kill recovery options (BleepingComputer Oct 7); Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra + Surface RTX Spark dev box (Nvidia's personal AI supercomputer chip goes mainstream); Google Labs Playground turns text prompts into playable games, no coding; Claude for Google Workspace beta (Docs/Sheets/Slides) for paid users
