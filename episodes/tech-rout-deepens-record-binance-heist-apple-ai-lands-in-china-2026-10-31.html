Tech Rout Deepens, Record Binance Heist, Apple AI Lands in China

Friday's tech rout wipes nearly a trillion dollars in a week, hackers pull off the largest crypto theft ever at Binance, and Apple Intelligence finally reaches China.

October 31, 2026 · 6:16 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Friday's market rout closes a brutal week for tech (Fri Oct 30 regular session)**: Nasdaq fell ~3%; nearly $1tn wiped off the Nasdaq in a week, ~$2tn across October; Intel fell close to 30% (worst day since 1974) on its weak Q4 forecast despite beating Q3 profit; Amazon fell on a profit miss and higher spending; Nvidia slipped ~3%; Bitcoin slid to ~$77,000. Follow-through, not a re-cover: yesterday's episode covered only the after-hours moves.
  • **Record $6.3B Binance heist (attack late Thu Oct 29, reported Fri Oct 30)**: Largest crypto theft ever, most of it drained in ~20 minutes across 10 blockchains; TRM Labs and Chainalysis attribute it with high confidence to North Korea's TraderTraitor group (same group blamed for the $1.5B Bybit theft, Feb 2025); Binance cited a compromised engineer's laptop plus additional breach points, froze withdrawals (restart expected within ~48 hours), ~$3B frozen on-chain, and set aside $6.34B+ to cover all customer losses. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Apple Intelligence comes to China with iOS 27.3, powered by Alibaba's Qwen (announced Fri Oct 30)**: Developer beta testing starts Nov 4, public release planned for December; includes Writing Tools, Visual Intelligence, Live Translation, Genmoji, Clean Up, and notification summaries; new Siri AI and Image Playground excluded; iPhone-only at launch (iPhone 15 Pro and newer), Mac/iPad pending further approvals. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes (Oct 29 episode covered the touchscreen MacBook Pro, a different Apple story).
