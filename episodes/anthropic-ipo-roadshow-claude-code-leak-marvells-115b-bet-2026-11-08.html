Anthropic IPO Roadshow, Claude Code Leak, Marvell's $115B Bet

Anthropic hits the road for the biggest AI IPO ever while OpenAI banks $11B, a Claude Code flaw leaks live credentials onto public GitHub, and Marvell bets $115B that optics are the future of AI data centers.

In this episode

  • **Anthropic IPO roadshow + OpenAI $11B raise (weekend Nov 7–8)**: Anthropic kicks off IPO roadshow week of Nov 9, Nasdaq ticker ANT, listing targeted before Thanksgiving (Nov 26); valuation $1.25–$1.75T, raise $80–$100B — down from $2T hopes after Thursday's selloff. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Marvell buys Lumentum for $115B (announced Sat Nov 7)**: All-stock deal, ~$1,132/share, one of the ten largest tech acquisitions on record; optics (lasers/transceivers, 400G/800G) for AI data-center clusters; ~64x Lumentum's ~$1.8B trailing revenue; >$1B run-rate synergies; close expected H1 2028. Consolidation context: IonQ closed $1.15B Elisity buy; Shield AI acquiring Echodyne for $5.3B. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Microsoft dismantles AI Frontiers research group (reported Nov 7–8)**: Flagship fundamental research group shuttered; co-founder John Langford left, saying fundamental research lost its place to model scaling; resources shift to product/scaling. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.

Huawei Qingyun HM650 (Nov 6) — first desktop PC built entirely with Chinese-made components, Kirin X90 chip, HarmonyOS. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.

So if you use Claude Code, search your organization for that settings file, add it to your project git ignore, and rotate any credential it ever held. Deleting the file does not help, because the secrets live on in git history and in forks.
