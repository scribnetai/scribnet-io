Nvidia Board Moves to Oust Huang, Anthropic IPO Book Fills

Nvidia's board reportedly prepares to oust Jensen Huang days before earnings, Anthropic's IPO book fills up on blockbuster S-1 numbers, Google ships a decision-making model, Apple previews a local AI server for Macs, and TSMC confirms an employee data leak.

November 12, 2026 · 7:18 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Nvidia board reportedly preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang (WSJ, Nov 11)**: Board met Nov 11 after NYT named a second woman, VP of Healthcare Kimberly Powell (a direct report), in a pattern of affairs. Outside firm engaged to identify successors; CFO Colette Kress likely interim CEO. Shares fell 8.6% after hours, below $160 for the first time since July 2025; $450–600B in value erased since the Nov 8 inquiry opened. Bernstein downgraded to market perform, price target halved to $200. Earnings Nov 17 — Kress may deliver them as interim. Also: WSJ reports Nvidia stopped breaking out Singapore revenue (22% of revenue) after quietly blocklisting customer Megaspeed in June 2025. Major escalation — Nov 9 episode covered only the investigation opening.
  • **Anthropic amended S-1: $33.24B revenue for first nine months, IPO book oversubscribed (Nov 11–12)**: Revenue up ~1,200% YoY; Q3 revenue $16.96B; run-rate revenue $81B at Oct 31, up $10B in one month. Book reportedly several times oversubscribed; pricing still Nov 18, Nasdaq debut now targeted Nov 20 (was Nov 19) at up to $1.4T. Nvidia and Alphabet each agreed to buy ~$10B of stock at IPO price as part of a $26B private placement. Infrastructure commitments ~$895.4B, about 14x the raise. Follow-up to Nov 11 pricing story — the S-1 figures, oversubscription, and Nov 20 date are new.
  • **Google launches Gemini Decisions API and Decision 1 model (Nov 11)**: Scores up to 1,000 yes/no, choice, or rubric questions in one forward pass, 150–250ms latency, with confidence scores; $0.10 per million input tokens. Third major lab to ship a dedicated decision model within ~six weeks (modeled on TypeSafe's Jev; same price as OpenAI's equivalent). Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Apple's Claris testing Harbor, a local AI server app for Mac (Nov 10–11)**: Turns Apple silicon Macs into a private AI server for open-weight models; pools unified memory across Macs over Thunderbolt 5; no cloud fees or per-token billing. Private preview with early-access sign-up; App Store listing spotted by leaker Aaron Perris Nov 10, then pulled. No price, release date, or supported-Mac list; Apple has not officially announced it. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Security: TSMC confirms employee data leak via third-party cloud provider (Nov 12)**: Hacker advertising ~46,000 employee records (6.9GB) for 3 BTC (~$220,000) on DarkForums/BreachForums; sample includes national IDs, home addresses, bank details, emergency contacts; threat to publish if unsold within a week. TSMC says internal systems not breached; production and customer data unaffected. Second supplier-side incident after the 2023 Kinmax/LockBit breach. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **DeepMind launches AlphaGenome 2 (Nov 11)**: DNA model trained on 100M+ single-cell profiles across 68 tissues; predicts how genetic variants behave in specific cell types. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Quick hits**: CoreWeave jumped 25% on results; semiconductor index up across the board (Micron and Sandisk each +11%+); Nintendo DMCA takedown removes 144 Switch modchip firmware repositories from GitHub (Picofly network, Nov 6 notice); Google Gemini app rolling out on Apple CarPlay starting Nov 11.
