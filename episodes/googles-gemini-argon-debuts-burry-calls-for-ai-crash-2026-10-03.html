Google's Gemini Argon Debuts, Burry Calls for AI Crash

Alex and Jordan break down Google's Gemini 4 Argon frontier model, Michael Burry's call for an AI market crash, Amazon's eight-billion-dollar chip leaseback, DeepSeek's first outside funding round at a monster valuation, and Meta's open-source Muse Gadgets.

October 03, 2026 · 6:37 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • Google Gemini 4 Argon (Oct 1): new frontier model claiming to beat OpenAI/Anthropic on software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, cybersecurity; 1M-token output limit (up from 64K), 77.9% on DeepSWE v1.1; $2/$10 per M tokens matching OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol; rolling out first to trusted cyber defenders via Fairwind program; Sundar Pichai quote; contrast with OpenAI shelving GPT-6.1 Astra over safety failures
  • Michael Burry AI crash call (Oct 3): "For the sake of humanity, the market must crash hard to stop OpenAI and Anthropic from going public" — fears they'd burn trillions; paired with Amazon's ~$8B Nvidia chip SPV/leaseback asset-light financing (chips stay in use, capital moves off books); Bain $6T AI revenue gap as the math behind the anxiety; framed as loudest AI bubble debate since dot-com
  • DeepSeek first external raise + Huawei partnership (Oct 1-3): ~50B yuan raise at ~500B yuan valuation, first outside money ever for the lab; DeepSeek-Huawei AI software partnership; DeepSeek models being refined on Huawei chips as China AI self-reliance story; full-stack (chips, frameworks, models) alternative to US supply chains
  • Meta Muse Gadgets open-source (Oct 3): open-source firmware + Linux SDK for DIY Muse-connected hardware; Raspberry Pi / ESP32 class boards, displays/sensors/actuators; Muse Home Link reference device with 5,000 free units; fits Meta Enterprise Platform play — assistant wars becoming hardware wars

Argon is rolling out first to a select group of what Google calls trusted cyber defenders, through a program called Fairwind, before expanding to developers, enterprises, and eventually consumers. Alex: That's a notable framing choice — a frontier model led with cyber defense. Google chief executive Sundar Pichai says it shows frontier performance in complex workflows, cyber defense, and software engineering. Michael Burry, the investor who became famous for predicting the two thousand eight housing crash, immortalized in The Big Short, posted on X yesterday with a doozy.
