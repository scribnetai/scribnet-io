Bring Back Huang, Cybercab Monthly Fee, Datacenter Protests

The campaign to bring Jensen Huang back to Nvidia gains steam, Tesla's Cybercab comes with a mandatory monthly fee, and thousands protest AI datacenters — plus a benchmark upset and a security roundup.

November 15, 2026 · 7:08 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Petition to reinstate Jensen Huang passes 115,000 signatures; Megaspeed chip re-export scandal widens (Nov 14)**: TechCrunch reports the reinstatement petition — signed by Elon Musk and other tech executives — passed 115,000 signatures three days after Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO. Separately, Malaysia opened a formal investigation into whether Megaspeed's local subsidiary illegally re-exported Nvidia chips in violation of US export controls; US Treasury involved, Commerce Dept. infighting reported. Reuters analysis of 150+ procurement notices: ~$4B in Chinese AI datacenter projects at risk from export restrictions and Nvidia leadership uncertainty. WSJ: Huang cultivated Southeast Asian partners/sites so Chinese firms could use Nvidia chips remotely. Fresh — Nov 12–14 episodes covered the resignation/investigation and the self-financed chip sales, not the petition or the Megaspeed probe.
  • **Thousands march in Edinburgh against large AI datacenters (Nov 14)**: BBC Scotland reports demonstrators marched to the Scottish Parliament opposing large AI datacenters, including a planned 1,000 MW Chapelcross site; concerns are electricity demand, construction disruption, and noise. Scottish government says planning guidance due before year end; sites over 50 MW must submit an Environmental Impact Assessment. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Gemini 4 Carbon tops MathArena at 15x the cost; Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2 / Mythos 5.2 (Nov 14)**: The Decoder reports Gemini 4 Carbon is the first non-OpenAI model in a while to lead MathArena, displacing GPT-6.1 Sol, with Claude Fable 5.2 second. Carbon costs ~15x Sol, Fable 5.2 ~40x. Anthropic also released Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 (claimed better performance, lower cost) and made Claude Opus 5.5 the default on Pro/Max/Ultra/Team plans with new prompting guidance. Fresh — Nov 11 episode covered the Gemini 4 Carbon launch, not the benchmark result or the new Claude models.
  • **Tesla opens invite-only Cybercab orders: $29,990 plus mandatory $299/month autonomy fee (Nov 13–14)**: TechCrunch/The Verge report selected customers can order the steering-wheel-free two-seater for personal use, deliveries estimated December 2026. Terms: operable only within Tesla Robotaxi geofences (Texas currently), no commercial use (including Tesla's own Robotaxi network; customer cars may join Q2 2027), fee non-cancellable without rendering the car unusable. Musk posted the $299 fee is too high and should be ~$115/month. NHTSA formal audit of the self-certification still pending. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Security roundup: Cursor backdoored Claude Code extension, North Korea FaceStealer $400M crypto theft, Osaka breach (Nov 13–14)**: Cursor's Nov 13 incident update traces its October breach to a Fireworks AI access key: malicious Anthropic Claude Code extension versions 2.1.312/2.1.313 in its marketplace cache for ~11 hours on Oct 29, up to 5,000 developers potentially received them; extensions attempted persistent SSH backdoors. Fewer than 0.05% of users had traces exposed, <0.01% had API credentials exposed; contained, last attacker activity Nov 1. FBI/DOJ: North Korean FaceStealer stole $400M+ in crypto over ~18 months via fake video calls/deepfakes; US seized underlying cloud infrastructure. City of Osaka: breach exposed 1M+ email addresses via third-party provider Arara (which disclosed ~77M emails across ~400 customers, incl. Sega). CISA added UnrealIRCd (CVE-2026-114713) and Check Point (CVE-2026-123039) flaws to KEV Nov 13, federal deadline Nov 16. WIRED/Halcyon: AI-assisted ransomware, ~111 groups claiming attacks Jul–Sep. Fresh detail — Nov 14 episode covered the Fireworks second breach and Check Point zero-day generally, not the Cursor marketplace backdoor specifics or FaceStealer/Osaka.
  • **Quick hits**: OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet Nov 13 (agents shop on a user's behalf). Google connected Gemini to Google Wallet (read-only bank account access for budgets/cash-flow charts, via Plaid). SpaceX caught a Starship upper stage for the first time. Gemini 4 Hydrogen now generally available in GitHub Copilot.
  • **Forward watch**: Nvidia Q3 earnings Tue Nov 17 (first without Huang; Kress presenting; ~$108B guidance); Anthropic IPO pricing Wed Nov 18, Nasdaq trading as ANT Thu Nov 19/20.
