Nvidia SEC Probe Sinks Stocks, Riemann Proof Certified

Nvidia faces an SEC accounting probe as the Nasdaq slides into a bear market, the Claude-assisted Riemann proof gets certified, and breaches hit Microsoft, Rackspace, and the FBI.

November 22, 2026 · 7:11 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Nvidia SEC accounting probe and the market rout (Nov 20)**: Reuters reported the SEC is examining Nvidia's accounting practices; shares fell 14% Friday to $125.53, down 23% on the week (worst since 2008), ~$2.5T shed from peak, down nearly 50% from the $243.37 October high. Nasdaq fell 5.28% Friday, 10% on the week, now in bear market territory (down 20%+ from peak) and has wiped out all 2026 gains; S&P 500 below 7,000. California AG subpoenaed Nvidia Thursday. Fresh — Nov 21 episode covered the Anthropic day-two stumble and general sell-off, not the SEC probe or bear-market close.
  • **Riemann Hypothesis proof certified by Lean/Mathlib (Nov 21-22)**: The Lean and Mathlib communities jointly certified Alpöge's Claude-assisted proof across a 17M-line library; The Economist declared AI may have solved math's most important problem; the rival all-human GRH proof was called invalid. Fresh follow-up — Nov 21 episode covered only the initial claim, with no verification yet.
  • **Security roundup: Microsoft Entra breach, Rackspace Spot, FBI NCIC, USDA, EU Commission AI-assisted attack, F5 patches (Nov 21-22)**: Attackers exploited an Entra Connect Health flaw to steal Microsoft tenant data; Rackspace Spot breached via compromised credentials with customer Kubernetes secrets accessed; FBI's National Crime Information Center reportedly breached (second FBI breach in two months); USDA cyber incident disrupted farmer payments; European Commission confirmed an AI-assisted cyberattack disrupted services; F5 issued emergency patches for five critical BIG-IP RCE flaws. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Quick hits**: OpenAI GPT-Live-1.1 voice model with 60% cheaper idle calls; Tencent open-sources Hy4, a 770B-parameter multimodal model with a million-token context; ByteDance Axion 1.0 open agent near frontier after three training stages; AI server makers (Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Supermicro) reportedly repricing signed deals up to 45% on memory costs.
