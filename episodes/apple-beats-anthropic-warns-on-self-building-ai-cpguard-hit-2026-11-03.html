Apple Beats, Anthropic Warns on Self-Building AI, cPGuard Hit

Apple posts a record quarter under John Ternus, Anthropic warns AI may soon improve itself faster than it can be evaluated, and a trusted security update channel is turned into an attack.

November 03, 2026 · 7:03 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Apple's fiscal Q4 2026 results (reported after the bell Mon Nov 2)**: Revenue $114.5B (+12%), profit $30.7B, EPS $2.10, all beating expectations; first earnings report under CEO John Ternus. iPhone revenue a record $57.58B (+17.5%) on Pro-only models; Mac $10.79B (+24%); services $31.57B in line; iPad down ~10% to $6.23B. Gross margin 47.9% beat, but holiday-quarter margin guidance of 45–46% disappointed on memory-chip costs and Siri investment. December-quarter revenue growth guided 7–10%, potentially Apple's biggest quarter ever; iPhone Duo still supply-constrained with no balance timeline. Follow-through, not a re-cover: Nov 2 episode only previewed the report.
  • **Anthropic warns AI may soon improve itself faster than it can be evaluated (amended IPO filing, Nov 2)**: Company says it may imminently cross its Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for dramatically accelerated AI-assisted research and might not detect the shift until afterward. Investors reportedly pushing valuation to $1.5–1.6T from $2T; quarterly revenue ~$17B with growth slowing sharply. Fresh — prior episodes covered Anthropic's Opus 5.5 launch and Amodei's White House meeting, not this RSI warning.
  • **MIT weight-space programming: one model edits another's weights (reported Nov 2–3)**: OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra edited 0.016% of Qwen3.5-0.8B's weights with no training run, lifting its MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1% in the researchers' account. Caveat covered in the episode: other researchers dispute the size of the effect, arguing it may be closer to removing a reasoning loop, and an independent rerun reportedly matched the gain with a simpler setting. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **cPGuard supply-chain attack via compromised update channel (began Nov 2, reported Nov 3)**: Attacker used compromised credentials to reach OPSSHIELD's update distribution infrastructure and pushed a malicious script through cPGuard versions 5.88.12–5.88.19. Script collected password hashes, root DB config, root shell history, and root SSH keys; vendor estimates the attacker retrieved 32 files, one per customer server. Fix: update to 5.88.25 or later and rotate exposed credentials. Fresh — not covered in prior episodes.
  • **Quick hits / week ahead**: US midterm elections Tue Nov 3, with OpenAI and Anthropic running dedicated election-misinformation teams; AMD earnings after the bell Tue Nov 3; monthly jobs report Fri; Apple Intelligence testing in China (Alibaba Qwen) starts Nov 4 in the iOS developer beta.
