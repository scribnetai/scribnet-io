Anthropic IPO Pops, vCenter Zero-Day, Card Data Leak

Anthropic closes its record-breaking first day up seventeen percent, a China-linked group exploited a critical VMware vCenter zero-day for months before the patch, and a third-party breach leaks Visa, Mastercard, and JPMorgan customer data — plus hands-on AI agents and Google's orbital servers.
