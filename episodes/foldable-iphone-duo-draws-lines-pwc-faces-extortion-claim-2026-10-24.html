Foldable iPhone Duo Draws Lines, PwC Faces Extortion Claim

Apple's foldable iPhone Duo launches to overnight lines, PwC pushes back on an extortion group's data claim, Capcom warns players about phishing, SpaceX recruits Grok partners, and Anthropic's draft IPO filing reveals huge revenue against even bigger compute commitments.

October 24, 2026 · 6:22 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Apple's first foldable iPhone Duo launches to overnight lines and tight supply (Fri Oct 23; 9to5Mac / USA Today / Softonic)**: Book-style foldable went on sale in Apple Stores worldwide Oct 23 after Oct 16 preorders. 7.6-inch inner display, 5.4-inch cover screen, titanium frame, A20 Pro, 48MP camera, Touch ID returns; starts at $1,999 — most expensive iPhone ever. Apple warned walk-in inventory would be limited; lines formed overnight (200–300 people reported at one Texas store); preorder shipping slipped to January 2027 within minutes. Angle: Apple enters a category Samsung is on its 8th generation of — the bet is hinge/screen durability over being first; analysts say Apple could lead foldables by 2027. Fresh — not covered in recent episodes.
  • **Anthropic draft S-1 still dominates the weekend: $16.3B H1 revenue vs $600B+ compute commitments (draft reviewed by WSJ, reported Oct 20–21 via Reuters; weekend analysis Oct 23)**: H1 2026 revenue $16.3B (up from $1.1B a year earlier); compute spend ~$9B in the half; ~$450B in mostly noncancelable cloud deals (Google ~$111B, AWS ~$110B, Microsoft ~$31B) plus $160B+ Google TPU/Broadcom and up to $150B more in talks, pushing totals past $600B (some tallies $750B). Net loss $80.7B, of which ~$72B is a non-cash convertible-note accounting charge. About one-fifth of revenue from just two customers. Public filing could come ahead of a possible November listing. Script framed it as a weekend-digest segment and tied it to yesterday's Meta spending story without re-covering Meta's figures. Fresh to the podcast — not in recent episodes' topics.
  • **PwC confirms vendor incident as Everest extortion group claims 590GB theft (Fri Oct 23; Reuters)**: PwC confirmed a cybersecurity incident involving a third-party vendor hosting employee learning records. Everest claimed on Oct 22 to have stolen 590GB (regional HR/learning exports, integration docs, names/emails/phones/addresses) with a 5-day countdown. PwC says the site holds no client data, sensitive personal data, or financial information, and its network/operations are unaffected. Script kept claim vs confirmation clearly separated. Fresh.
  • **Capcom updates Capcom ID breach: 11,774,247 accounts exposed, phishing is the main risk (update Fri Oct 23; Capcom notice via BleepingComputer / Cyber Security News)**: Unauthorized access detected Oct 19, reported to police and Japan's PPC Oct 21. Exposed for ~11.77M accounts: email, account creation/last-login dates, login counts, last-login IP; 43 accounts also had DOB, gender, country/region, nickname. Passwords changed without authorization on 196 accounts (already reset); Capcom says no evidence customer-set passwords leaked and no card data involved. Games/services remain up. Fresh.
  • **SpaceX launches Grok enterprise partner network with 130+ partners (announced Thu Oct 22; PYMNTS Oct 23)**: SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network splits into transformation partners (deploy Grok) and marketplace partners (plugins/integrations); launch partners include Bain & Company, Databricks, Nvidia, Snowflake. Grok Bot workplace agent reportedly passed ~418,000 weekly users as of mid-September, about a month after launch. Angle: enterprise AI race shifting from model scores to partner channels. Fresh.
