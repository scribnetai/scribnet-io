Nvidia Closes the Checkbook, Anthropic IPO Week Begins

Nvidia's interim CEO moves to rein in the chip giant's deal financing as Google's TPUs win huge orders, Anthropic heads for a trillion-dollar Nasdaq debut, ChatGPT agents start spending your money, and ransomware wipes a VMware estate without exploiting a single flaw.

November 16, 2026 · 6:53 · with Alex & Jordan

In this episode

  • **Nvidia closes the checkbook (WSJ, Nov 16)**: Interim CEO Colette Kress expected to rein in Nvidia's aggressive AI deal financing; follows the ~$530B commitments/guarantees story — new angle is the pullback under Kress, who cut a proposed OpenAI guarantee from ~$250B to $125B in August. Market value down from ~$5.8T in early October to ~$3.9T on Friday; stock fell ~13% last week, worst since March 2025.
  • **Google TPUs gain ground (Ming-Chi Kuo, Nov 16)**: OpenAI seeking ~12M TPUs across 2028–2029, Anthropic ~14M; TPU shipment visibility extends to 2029 vs Nvidia GPUs mostly through 2027–2028; Nvidia leadership uncertainty helping Google pitch post-2028 plans.
  • **Nvidia Q3 earnings Tue Nov 17 (preview)**: Kress presenting; analysts expect ~$109B revenue, ~90% profit growth.
  • **Anthropic IPO week (pricing Wed Nov 18, trading Thu Nov 19, Nasdaq ANT)**: Valuation trimmed to $1.3–1.4T from $1.5–2T discussed weeks ago; book ~6x oversubscribed; ~$900.9B outstanding commitments; Google to own ~13% after listing; Q3 revenue ~$17B, up 47% QoQ vs 144% in Q2.
  • **ChatGPT Wallet launches (OpenAI, Nov 13)**: Agents (dots) can now complete purchases; rolling out to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers 18+ with eligible US-issued cards; built with Amex, Mastercard, Visa; per-purchase approval or pre-authorized limits; agents never see full card details; no transaction cut at launch.
  • **United Airlines restricts unauthorized AI agents (Nov 11)**: Citing credential exposure, unwanted booking changes, check-in declarations requiring human acknowledgment; Amazon earlier blocked Meta's Muse agent — the industry splits on agentic commerce.
  • **BlackNevas ransomware wipes VMware estate (Securelist, Nov 13)**: 10-day intrusion destroyed 4 ESXi hosts, all VMs, 2 vCenter appliances and backups using legitimate management interfaces and stolen admin credentials — no vulnerability exploited; antivirus in backup environment disabled days earlier.
  • **Quick hits**: Alibaba launches Qwen4-Max (MoE, native vision, 1M-token context, $1.50/$4.50 per million tokens, preview pricing until Nov 25); Gemini 4 Carbon price doubles Dec 1 ($2/$10 → $4/$20 per million tokens); Congress moves to tighten AI chip export rules after Huang scandal; 11:11 Systems survey — 89% of UK orgs report rising data center infra costs (avg +14%), 73% hit by at least one cyberattack/breach in the past year.
